GUIDES & PRODUCTS

Turning trust into products.

The DMs kept asking the same questions. I packaged the answers instead of answering one at a time. The broad guide is free for every incoming student; the deep-dive guides sit behind it, sold through content that gives part of the solution away.

FREE · 16 PAGES · WRITTEN & DESIGNED SOLO

The UC Davis Starter Guide — 25 things I wish I knew before coming to Davis.

The flagship free guide: first week, getting around, campus vocabulary, apps, food, and the bucket list — written the way I'd say it over coffee at the CoHo, not the way a brochure would. It's the top of the funnel for every paid guide below it.
